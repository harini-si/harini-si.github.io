I am a research associate at Adobe MDSR. Currently, my research focuses on developing agents with the ability to reason and generate hypotheses in dynamic, open-ended tasks.
Previously, I obtained my bachelor's in CS from BITS Pilani, Goa. For my undergraduate thesis, I worked on "text-to-simulation-code" generation with Dr. Nick Haber at Stanford. At BITS, I was mentored by Dr. Ashwin Srinivasan and Dr. Gautam Shroff, where I explored neurosymbolic techniques to solve the ARC challenge.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading,travelling and badminton.
