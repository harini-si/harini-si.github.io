S I Harini

I am a Research Associate 2 at Adobe MDSR. My research explores how AI can help us understand and predict human behavior—and how insights about human behavior can, in turn, make AI systems more effective. I pursue this through two complementary threads: developing AI systems that learn from human behavior to understand, predict, and optimize how people respond to content, and developing LLM-based agents that can accelerate scientific discovery by uncovering novel, empirically grounded theories.
Previously, I earned my bachelor's in CS from BITS Pilani, Goa, where my undergraduate thesis with Prof. Nick Haber and Fan-Yun Sun at Stanford focused on generating end-to-end game and robotics environments from natural-language input to train reinforcement learning agents. At BITS, I was mentored by Dr. Ashwin Srinivasan and Dr. Gautam Shroff, where I explored RL and neurosymbolic techniques to solve the ARC-AGI challenge.
